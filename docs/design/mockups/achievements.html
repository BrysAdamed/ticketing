Achievements

Ticketing mockup · TKT-92 for TKT-66, TKT-67, TKT-68, TKT-93, TKT-98 · D-144

Private recognition for real actions. Everything here is visible only to its owner, Admins included, until the owner shares a single medal; others then see its name, tier and medal, never when it was earned. No totals, ranks, comparisons or anything time-based. The whole surface is hidden when the achievements flag is off for the viewer or the user opted out.

Medal: anatomy, sizes and states

tk-achievement-medal in shared/ui · design-system §8 "Achievement medal"
  1. Ring: hexagon outline in the tier colour, 2.5 units at the 48 grid. One-time medals get a thin ring in the palette colour instead.
  2. Body: hexagon filled with --tk-palette-n mixed at --tk-tint-avatar over the surface, 1.25 stroke in --tk-palette-n. n = hash(id) mod 8, like labels and avatars.
  3. Glyph: the Tabler icon from the catalogue, 18 of 48, stroked in --tk-palette-n-fg.
  4. Pips: one dot per tier reached (bronze 1 to platinum 4), so tier never depends on colour. Drawn at 32px and up; below that the tier is written next to the medal.
