Show saved cards in checkout wallet

As a returning customer I want to see my saved cards at checkout, so that I can pay without typing my card details again.

Acceptance criteria

  • Saved cards are listed above the new-card form, most recently used first
  • Each card shows the brand, the last four digits and the expiry date
  • Expired cards are shown disabled, with an Update link
  • The customer can remove a card (asks for confirmation)

Notes

Cards come from vault v3 via GET /vault/v3/customers/{id}/cards. Never render the full PAN; see PAY-131. Designs: Checkout wallet, Figma.
